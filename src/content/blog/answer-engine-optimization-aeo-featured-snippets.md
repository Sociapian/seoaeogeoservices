Ask Google "how long does SEO take" and the first thing you often see is not a link but a short answer pulled from someone's page. Ask a voice assistant the same question and it will read one answer aloud. **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** is the work of making that answer yours.

## What is AEO?

Answer Engine Optimization is the practice of structuring content so search engines and assistants can use it as a direct answer. It targets:

- **Featured snippets**: the answer box above the regular results.
- **People Also Ask**: the expandable list of related questions.
- **Voice answers** from Google Assistant, Siri and Alexa.
- **AI summaries** such as Google AI Overviews, which also draw on clear, well-structured answers.

AEO does not replace SEO. A page usually needs to rank on the first page before it is considered for an answer box. AEO is what turns a ranking into *the* answer.

## The three answer formats

Google picks a format that suits the question. Before writing, search your target question and note which format appears.

| Format | Typical questions | What to write |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paragraph | "What is…", "Why does…", "Is it worth…" | A 40 to 60 word definition or explanation directly under the heading |
| List | "How to…", "Steps to…", "Best ways to…" | A numbered list for steps, bullets for options, each item starting with the key phrase |
| Table | "X vs Y", "Price of…", "Comparison of…" | A simple table with clear column headings |

## Step 1: Find the questions people actually ask

Good AEO starts with real questions, not guesses. Collect them from:

- **People Also Ask boxes** for your main topics. Each answer you open reveals more questions.
- **Google Search Console**: filter queries containing words like *how*, *what*, *why*, *cost* and *best*.
- **Your sales and support teams**: the questions prospects ask before buying are the ones they also type into search.
- **Autocomplete** in Google and YouTube.

Group the questions by topic and decide which page should answer each group. One page should own each question.

## Step 2: Write answer-first

Under each question heading, use this template:

1. **Restate and answer** in the first sentence. ("SEO usually takes three to six months to show meaningful results.")
2. **Qualify** in one or two sentences: what it depends on.
3. **Stop at roughly 40 to 60 words.** That is the block most likely to be lifted.
4. **Then go deeper** with examples, steps, data and nuance for readers who keep going.

Phrase the heading as the question itself, using the same words your audience uses. "How long does SEO take?" beats "Timelines and expectations".

## Step 3: Make the structure obvious

Search engines rely on clean HTML to understand which text answers which question:

- One `H1`, then `H2` headings for main questions and `H3` for sub-questions.
- Real HTML lists (`<ol>`, `<ul>`) and tables (`<table>`), not images of tables or styled paragraphs.
- Short paragraphs and descriptive subheadings.
- No key answers hidden inside tabs or accordions that only load with JavaScript.

## Step 4: Use schema, with the right expectations

Structured data helps machines understand what your page contains. For AEO the most relevant types are `FAQPage`, `HowTo`, `Article`, `Organization` and `BreadcrumbList`.

Be aware of two changes Google made in 2023: **FAQ rich results** are now shown only for a small set of well-known, authoritative government and health websites, and **HowTo rich results** were removed. The markup can still help engines interpret your content, but most sites should not expect it to produce extra visual features in results. The visible answer on the page is what wins the snippet.

## Step 5: Optimise for voice

Voice answers are usually read from featured snippets or local business data. To improve your chances:

- Write answers in natural, spoken language.
- Keep the key answer short enough to read aloud comfortably.
- For local businesses, keep your Google Business Profile complete and consistent: hours, address, phone, categories.

## How to measure AEO

- **Snippets owned**: track which target questions show your page in the answer box (most rank-tracking tools report this).
- **People Also Ask presence**: how many of your target questions include your page.
- **Search Console**: impressions and click-through rate for question queries.

Snippets change hands often. Review your key answers every few months and refresh them when a competitor takes over.
